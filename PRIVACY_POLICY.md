# Privacy Policy for Quick Weather View Extension

**Last Updated:** August 31, 2025

Thank you for using the Quick Weather View Chrome Extension ("the Extension"). This policy explains what information the Extension handles and how it is used.

**1. Information Handled by the Extension**

The Extension requires certain information provided by you to function correctly:

- **Saved Cities List:** The list of city names you add manually (via popup input or context menu) is stored **only locally** on your computer using Chrome's `chrome.storage.local` API.
- **Home City:** The city name you designate as your 'Home City' in the options is stored **only locally** on your computer using Chrome's `chrome.storage.local` API.
- **Temperature Unit Preference:** Your choice of Celsius or Fahrenheit is stored **only locally** on your computer using Chrome's `chrome.storage.local` API.

**2. Weather Data Access**

- **API Service:** This extension includes built-in access to weather data through OpenWeatherMap's API service. The API access is managed by the extension developer, so you don't need to obtain your own API key.
- **Data Requests:** When you request weather information for cities, the city names are sent directly to OpenWeatherMap's servers ([https://openweathermap.org/](https://openweathermap.org/)) to retrieve current weather data. We recommend reviewing OpenWeatherMap's privacy policy regarding how they handle this data.

**3. Data Collection by the Developer**

The developer of Quick Weather View **does not collect, remotely store, track, share, or sell any personal information or user data**, including your city lists or browsing activity. The Extension functions entirely within your browser, and all your preferences are stored locally on your device.

**4. Browsing History and User Activity**

The Extension **does not** access, track, or store your web browsing history or any other user activity (like clicks or keystrokes).

**5. Changes to This Privacy Policy**

This policy may be updated occasionally. Any changes will be reflected in the updated policy available within the Extension's description or accompanying documentation.

**6. Contact Us**

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via thinknovatech@gmail.com.
